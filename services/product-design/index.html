Product & UI/UX Design.

Good interfaces are decisions, not decoration. I design products that are usable on day one and coherent on day one hundred.

My design work sits close to engineering, so what I hand over is buildable. I design with real content and real constraints, prototype early, and test with people before committing.

Approach

  • Discover. Understand the user, the job to be done and the metric that matters.
  • Structure. Turn ambiguity into flows, hierarchy and a content model before pixels.
  • Design. Explore in Figma, converge on a direction, and build a component-based system.
  • Validate. Clickable prototypes and quick usability checks before handoff.

Deliverables

  • Figma files with organised, named components and variants.
  • A reusable design system with tokens, states and documentation.
  • Clickable prototypes for stakeholder and user testing.
  • Developer-ready specs and redlines, plus a handover walkthrough.

Typical timeline

Two to six weeks depending on scope, with a checkpoint every week.
