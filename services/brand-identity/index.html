Brand Identity.

A brand is a promise you can recognise. I build identities with a clear idea behind them, not just a mark.

My design roots are in typography and visual systems, and brand work is where that shows. I build identities that stay coherent across every size and surface, from a 16-pixel favicon to a launch banner.

Approach

  • Position. Clarify who the brand is for and what it stands for.
  • Idea. Find the single concept the visual system can hang on.
  • Craft. Design the mark, type, colour and supporting graphics as one system.
  • Systemise. Document usage so the brand stays coherent without me.

Deliverables

  • Primary and secondary logo suite with clear-space and misuse rules.
  • Colour, typography and layout guidelines.
  • Production assets in every format you need (SVG, PNG, favicon, social).
  • A brand book your team can hand to anyone.

Typical timeline

Two to five weeks, including two structured review rounds.

Related work

Identities for Raster, Payvault, STRĪKE and Terra, shown in the design grid.
