AI Product Engineering.

AI should make a product reliably better, not just more impressive in a demo. I build the version that survives real users.

I have shipped AI into real products, from an on-device recommendation system to a hackathon-winning community-safety platform. I focus on the unglamorous parts that make AI trustworthy: evaluation, guardrails, latency and cost.

Approach

  • Frame. Identify the task AI actually improves and the metric to prove it.
  • Prototype. A fast, honest baseline before any fancy architecture.
  • Harden. Guardrails, prompt-injection defence, spend caps and human fallbacks.
  • Evaluate. A small eval set you can run in CI, so quality never regresses silently.

Deliverables

  • A working AI feature integrated into your product.
  • An evaluation harness and a documented failure-mode list.
  • Cost and usage controls with alerting.
  • Clear notes on what the model can and cannot do.

Typical timeline

Two to eight weeks for a focused feature, longer for platforms.

Sentry (Gemma-powered safety intelligence) and my notes on AI experiments in the browser.
