Where it started

I began with design in 2022, typography, layout and visual systems. Learning to make things look intentional taught me to see structure before surface, a habit that still shapes how I engineer software.

From screens to systems

Freelancing from 2023 put my work in front of real clients and real constraints. I quickly learned that a beautiful screen is only half the job, so I went deeper into software engineering to build complete products, not just interfaces. By 2025 I had launched my first independent product and learned what it takes to ship, fail and improve in public.

What I build now

Today I work at the intersection of design, engineering and AI. My favourite problems are the hard, unglamorous ones: keeping coursework moving with no internet (MeshLearn), making payments verifiable offline (Pulse), and helping a campus respond to emergencies faster (S.A.F.E.). In 2026, an AI community-safety platform I built won the Build with Gemma hackathon (Google DeepMind).

How I work

Understand the actual problem, frame it as a system, design the experience, engineer it for real, ship it, then iterate on what happens. Whether I am shaping a brand or shipping a web app, the goal is the same: build something people actually want to use.
