Privacy Policy

The short version: this website is a static portfolio and it collects as little information about you as possible.

What this site stores

This site has no accounts, no databases and no server-side application code. Pages are served as static files. The only thing stored in your browser is a single local preference (your light/dark theme choice) using localStorage. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time.

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, and there is no cookie consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

What we do not collect

  • No personal profiles or account data.
  • No payment information on this site.
  • No sale or sharing of visitor data with third parties.

Email

If you email me, I receive the information you choose to send (your address and message). I use it only to reply and to discuss potential work, and I do not add you to any mailing list without asking.

Third-party services

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts and may link out to external services (GitHub, LinkedIn, X, hosted demos). When your browser requests those resources, the relevant provider receives standard technical data such as your IP address, as described in their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Because we hold essentially no data about you, there is little to access or delete. If you have emailed me and want that correspondence removed, contact me and I will delete it.
