MeshLearn Offline learning over BLE mesh

2025

Runner-up · KWASU Tech Conference Founder · Full-stack engineer

  • Reframed slow downloads as a distribution problem: one connected phone seeds lectures to nearby phones over Bluetooth Low Energy.
  • Content moves in hash-verified chunks; a bad chunk is re-requested from another peer.
  • Built a conflict-resolution sync layer so offline progress is never overwritten when a device reconnects.
  • Built the React Native app and mesh logic end to end, and trained a TensorFlow Lite recommendation model.

React NativeBLE meshTensorFlow Lite

PayVault Offline payments protocol

2026

Concept & case study · Product design, architecture, brand

  • Designed a protocol where two devices commit to a payment with ECDSA signatures and escrow, then reconcile with the settlement network once signal returns.
  • Split the system into seven layers (signing, escrow, transport, queuing, settlement and more) so each can be reasoned about and replaced on its own.
  • Created the brand identity and marketing site for banks, PSPs and mobile money operators.
