A point of viewWhy I build for hard conditions

From no signal

Most of what I build has to survive bad networks, tight budgets and real emergencies. Lectures that travel phone to phone. Payments that settle offline. An SOS that hops over Bluetooth until it finds a signal. Making that feel effortless is the job.
