FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS.

What does a typical engagement look like?

Most projects run in focused sprints. We start with a short discovery call to define the actual problem, then I deliver a scoped plan with milestones. You get a working build to react to early and often, rather than one big reveal at the end.

Do you work with early-stage founders?

Yes. Several of my favourite projects (MeshLearn, Sentry) started as rough ideas. I can help you go from concept to a credible, shippable product, and I will tell you honestly when something is not worth building yet.

Can you join an existing engineering team?

Absolutely. I have contributed to larger codebases and design systems, and I am comfortable slotting into existing tooling, review processes and release cycles as a contract engineer.

What is your availability and response time?

I take on a limited number of projects at a time so each gets real attention. I reply to every inquiry within two business days, usually faster, and I will always give you a clear yes or no.

Do you handle launch, hosting and maintenance?

Yes. I ship production-ready builds with CI, hosting, analytics and monitoring wired up, and I offer a handover or an ongoing maintenance arrangement depending on what you need.
