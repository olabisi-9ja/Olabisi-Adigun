FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Still unsure? Ask me or send a brief.

What kind of work do you take on?

Websites, web and mobile products, brand identities, design systems, and AI features. I can design and build, or do either on its own.

Do you only design, or do you build too?

Both. I don’t hand over a Figma file and wish you luck. I build what I design, so it has to work in production, not just in a prototype.

How do we start?

Fill in the project questionnaire. It’s six quick questions, the answers land in my inbox, and I reply by email.

Where are you based? Do you work remotely?

I’m based in Nigeria, I travel a lot, and I work remotely with teams anywhere.

What should I have ready?

A rough idea of the problem, who it’s for, when you want to start, and any brand or product that already exists. The questionnaire covers all of it.
