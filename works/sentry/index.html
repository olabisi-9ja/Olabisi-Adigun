A hackathon project that became a first-place finish: an AI-powered community safety and emergency intelligence platform that analyzes incident reports, generates actionable insights and improves emergency response.

02The pivot

From a community brain feed to an award-winning safety tool.

Sentry wasn’t the original plan. Initially, I was building a real-time “community brain” feed. But as the hackathon progressed, I realized there was a much more pressing problem: campus safety and emergency response.

I decided to pivot entirely. I redesigned and rebuilt the entire platform in just one night, using Google DeepMind’s Gemma 4 for intelligent incident triage. The result was a first-place finish at the Build with Gemma: GenAI for SDGs hackathon.

03Interface
