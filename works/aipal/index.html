AiPal

Website design for AiPal Assist, an AI assistant. Calm, device-first, and live on its own domain.

01The brief

AiPal Assist is an AI assistant, and it needed a product site of its own: somewhere that explains what the assistant does, shows it working, and points people to the app.

I designed the site, which is now live at aipalassist.com .

02Decisions

Lead with the product

Every section shows the real interface on a laptop, tablet or phone, so the page explains itself before the copy has to.

A calm palette

Deep green and warm cream instead of the usual AI purple. The promise on the page is “Feel better. One moment at a time.”, and the colours had to agree with it.

Voice first

The assistant’s home screen is one control, “Tap to talk”, under a single question: “How can I help?” The site keeps that simplicity: one idea per section, one action per screen.
