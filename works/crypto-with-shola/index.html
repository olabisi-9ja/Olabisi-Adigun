Crypto with Shola

A redesign of Crypto with Shola’s website, rebuilt around what a first-time visitor needs to know: what she teaches, who it’s for, and where to begin.

01The starting point

Shola teaches crypto: trading signals, the mindset that trading takes, and the security basics most beginners skip. Her site was already live, and it was doing a lot of jobs at once.

The redesign starts from one question: can a first-time visitor tell, within a few seconds, what she offers and where they should begin?

02Decisions

Three pillars, side by side

Trading signals & tutorials, mindset & motivation, and broader education & security sit together on the first screen, so every visitor can find their lane without scrolling.

One loud accent

A single bright lime against black and cream. In a category full of neon charts and gradients, one colour used with restraint reads as more trustworthy.

A headline with a stance

“Built for the real world.” Crypto education is crowded with promises; this line says the lessons are practical, and the layout backs it up by getting straight to them.
